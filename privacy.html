USBAlias privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

USBAlias has no account, advertising or tracking. It can send a small set of anonymous diagnostics only when you turn on sharing.

What the app accesses, and why

  • USB serial devices. USBAlias asks macOS which USB serial devices are attached so it can match them against the rules you create. It reads device names (product, manufacturer, serial number) and the device path such as /dev/cu.usbmodem1234. It never opens, reads or writes data on the serial device itself.
  • Files you choose. If you pick an aliases folder, or a custom sound file, USBAlias remembers that choice so it can keep working after a restart. The app only writes inside the aliases folder you choose, and it only reads sound files you choose.
  • Application preferences. Rules, sounds and settings are stored locally in the app's own data folder (~/Library/Application Support/USB Alias/, inside the app's sandbox container for the App Store version). They stay on your Mac. Deleting the app and that folder removes them.

Anonymous diagnostics (first-run choice)

On first launch, USBAlias asks whether to share anonymous diagnostics. Nothing is sent before you answer, and “Don't Share” is the default button. You can change the choice later in Settings → General → Privacy. An existing explicit choice is preserved when you upgrade.

When sharing is on, USBAlias sends:

  • a random installation ID generated by the app;
  • app ID, version, build, platform and processor architecture;
  • anonymous launch and running-heartbeat records, uptime, active-day count and the number of aliases currently linked;
  • crash and error fingerprints, without messages or stack traces; and
  • coarse country or region derived by the network provider from the request.

These records go to the developer's self-hosted service at https://updates.reabow.co/telemetry/. They are used to understand whether the app is installed and running and to group reliability failures. Usage records are retained for 90 days and crash records for 30 days. USBAlias does not sell the data, use it for advertising, track you across apps or websites, or link it to an account.

Turning sharing off stops future reports and deletes queued reports and the random installation ID from the Mac.

What the diagnostics never include

  • USB product names, manufacturer names, serial numbers or /dev/cu.* paths.
  • Alias names or paths, rule names or rule criteria.
  • File names or paths, custom sound content or log message text.
  • Raw errors, stack traces, contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, camera or browsing data.
  • Advertising identifiers, account information or tracking identifiers.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, cross-app tracking or sale of data.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, photos, location, microphone, camera or browsing data.
  • No third-party SDKs.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, macOS asks for permission and shows the alerts locally. No notification content leaves your Mac.

Sharing

Apart from diagnostics you explicitly enable, nothing is shared with anyone, including the developer.

Children

The app is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
